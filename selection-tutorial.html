🔍 Selection Sort Tutorial

Selection Sort works by:

✅ Finding the minimum value
✅ Swapping it to the correct position
✅ Repeating for remaining elements

Example

i 50
20
80
min 10

🔍 Find the minimum value: 10

🔁 Swap it with: 50

After Swap

10
20
80
50

✅ First position sorted!

🎮 Gameplay Rules

✅ Click the minimum value

❌ Wrong clicks reduce accuracy

🏆 Get 100% to unlock next level
